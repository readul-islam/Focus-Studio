## 1. Executive summary

**Focuspilot** is an AI-powered operating system for architecture and interior design studios. Unlike most competitors that specialise in one layer (design presentation, accounting, or generic PM), Focuspilot unifies **studio operations, finance, procurement, CRM, AI tools, and four stakeholder portals** (studio, client, contractor, supplier) in a single monorepo platform.

### Current maturity

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Overall** | Advanced MVP (~80% core complete) |
| **Production infra** | Live on Vercel + AWS |
| **Market readiness** | Private beta ready; public beta needs gap closure |
| **Differentiation** | Strong on multi-portal + UK/Xero + AI + contractor field access |
| **Biggest blockers to launch** | Broader test coverage, mobile polish, GTM validation |

### One-line pitch (market)

> **Focuspilot** replaces 5–6 disconnected tools with one AI-native platform built for design studios — projects, procurement, Xero finance, contractor QR access, and client portals in a single system.

### Honest verdict vs market

Focuspilot is **not yet** a top-rated tool on G2/Capterra — no public reviews, v0.1.0, and several competitor table-stakes features remain partial. It **can** become a category leader by leaning into its unique four-sided platform and UK-first finance stack, while closing design-tool and accounting-depth gaps that Programa, DesignFiles, and Studio Designer own today.

## 2. Product identity & positioning

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Name** | Focuspilot |
| **Category** | Vertical SaaS — design studio operations |
| **Primary ICP** | UK mid-market studios (10–50 people) |
| **Secondary ICP** | Boutique studios (2–10), EU/APAC expansion |
| **Buyer** | Studio owner, ops manager, finance director |
| **Budget band** | £3–10k/year per studio |
| **Replacing** | Notion/Excel + Xero + email + spreadsheets + generic PM |

### Positioning statement

For **interior design and architecture studios** who lose margin to fragmented tools and poor field coordination, **Focuspilot** is the **all-in-one studio OS** that connects projects, procurement, finance, and every stakeholder portal — unlike Programa (spec-first), Studio Designer (accounting-first), or Houzz Pro (construction-first).

### Where to compete (wedge markets)

1. **UK studios on Xero** — native integration; competitors skew US/QuickBooks
2. **Studios with heavy contractor/trade coordination** — dedicated contractor PWA + QR codes + insurance tracking
3. **Studios wanting AI without bolt-ons** — clipper, daily brief, design studio, meeting summaries in-product
4. **Multi-stakeholder projects** — client + contractor + supplier portals in one vendor relationship

### Where not to compete (yet)

1. **US full-ledger accounting replacement** — Design Manager / Studio Designer own this
2. **E-design / moodboard-first solo designers** — DesignFiles wins on price and creative tooling
3. **Remodel/construction GMs** — Houzz Pro wins on takeoffs, daily logs, lead gen
4. **Enterprise spec libraries at scale** — Programa's trade portal and schedule maturity lead

### Primary competitors (direct)

| Competitor | Founded / HQ | Price (2026) | Core strength | Typical weakness |
|------------|--------------|--------------|---------------|------------------|
| **[Programa](https://programa.design)** | 2020, Australia | ~$47–59/user/mo | FF&E schedules, clipper, client dashboards, QR, mood boards | PM feels secondary; multi-currency/accounting limits |

| **[Design Manager](https://www.designmanager.com)** | US, established | $79/user/mo | Full ledger, spec-to-PO-to-invoice, profitability | US-only optimisations; dated UX; steep learning curve |
| **[Houzz Pro](https://pro.houzz.com)** | US (Ivy merger) | From ~$149/mo + ads | Construction PM, takeoffs, 3D plans, lead gen, subs | Overkill for pure design studios; expensive |
| **[DesignFiles](https://designfiles.co)** | US | $49–75/mo (+$25/user) | Moodboards, 3D, floor plans, e-design packages | Weak full-service procurement / trade pricing |

### Secondary competitors

| Competitor | Role in market |
|------------|----------------|
| **Mydoma** (Studio Designer family) | Client experience, mood boards — $64/user/mo |
| **Monday.com / Asana / Notion** | Generic PM — cheap but no FF&E/procurement depth |
| **QuickBooks + spreadsheets** | Status quo Focuspilot replaces |
| **Dubsado / HoneyBook** | Workflow CRM — no specs or procurement |

### Market trends (2025–2026)

1. **Consolidation** — Studio Designer acquired Mydoma; buyers want fewer vendors
2. **AI expectations** — clipper, inbox summarisation, and meeting notes becoming table stakes (Programa, Houzz Pro investing)
3. **Client portals with QR** — on-site access is now common (Programa, Houzz Pro market this)
4. **Per-user pricing fatigue** — studios push back above $60–80/seat; flat studio pricing is a positioning opportunity
5. **UK/EU gap** — most leaders are US/AU; Xero-native UK positioning is underserved

### What reviewers praise (across category)

- Product clipper / web sourcing
- Client portals and branded presentations
- Spec sheets linked to procurement
- Time tracking tied to projects
- QuickBooks/Xero sync (region-dependent)

### What reviewers complain about (across category)

- Steep onboarding and training costs
- Per-seat pricing at scale
- Clunky task/project management vs dedicated PM tools
- US-only accounting assumptions
- Weak mobile experiences
- Poor contractor/subcontractor workflows

**Focuspilot opportunity:** solve the complaint list where incumbents are weak — UK finance, contractor field access, mobile parity, flat pricing — while matching clipper, portal, and reporting table stakes.

## 6. Competitive strengths

These are **defensible differentiators** verified in the codebase — lead with them in market copy, demos, and comparison pages.

### 1. Four-sided platform (rare in category)

Only Focuspilot ships **studio + client + contractor + supplier** portals from one monorepo. Competitors typically offer studio + client only. This matches how real projects actually run.

### 2. Contractor field-first access

- Permanent **6-character access codes** (e.g. `JFLT-01`)
- **QR codes** per project
- Dedicated **contractor PWA** with doc sharing, procurement visibility, insurance/cert expiry tracking
- No competitor matches this depth for on-site trades

### 3. UK / Xero-native finance positioning

While US tools push QuickBooks or proprietary ledgers, Focuspilot integrates **Xero** at the core — a clear wedge for UK studios (VAT, GBP, familiar bookkeeper workflows).

### 4. AI woven into operations (not a bolt-on)

| Tool | Function |
|------|----------|
| AI Clipper | URL → structured product → library → procurement |
| AI Inbox | Gmail sync, thread analysis, suggested actions |
| Daily Brief | Morning studio summary |
| Design Studio | 2D vision/generation + Meshy 3D GLB |
| Meetings | Vexa bot → transcript → action items |

Programa has clipper; Houzz has schedule AI; neither matches this breadth.

### 5. Industry-specific reporting (6 pages, PDF export)

Overview, Projects, Team (hours/utilisation/timesheet), Finance, Procurement, Revenue & P&L — built for **design studio economics**, not generic PM dashboards.

### 6. Presentation builder with client engagement

Canvas editor, product pins, client comments, public share links — bridges sales and delivery.

### 7. Automation & extensibility

- Zapier + `fp_live_` API keys
- Notion two-way sync with board/Gantt creation
- Public studio profiles at `focuspilot.io/studio/{slug}`

### 8. Mobile parity ambition

Expo app with offline cache covering projects, CRM, finance, reports, inbox — unusual depth for v0.1 vs competitors' mobile-light approaches.

### 9. Modern stack & deployment

Next.js 14 + Django 5 + Turbo monorepo; live on Vercel/AWS — faster iteration than legacy desktop-era competitors.

### 10. Help centre maturity

58 in-app articles, feedback API, screenshot galleries — reduces support load and builds trust pre-launch.
